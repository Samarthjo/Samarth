From idea to operating model

The project was structured as an end-to-end entrepreneurship plan rather than a standalone product mock-up.

01 · CUSTOMER

Health + environment

The target market was framed around consumers who value health, environmental safety, and are willing to pay a premium for natural alternatives.

02 · PRODUCT

Natural differentiation

The concept used natural ingredients and essential oils to differentiate from chemical-based fresheners, with multiple product-line ideas for different use cases.

03 · GO-TO-MARKET

Multi-channel reach

The marketing plan combined product, price, promotion and place, with social platforms, influencer-led awareness and broad distribution access.

Strategic analysis

The report examined the venture through market, competitive, operational and contingency lenses.

SWOT

Where the concept could win

Strengths included natural-ingredient differentiation, premium-margin potential and rising awareness of chemical-free products. Risks included raw-material availability, manufacturing complexity, established competitors and supply-chain interruptions.

PORTER'S FIVE FORCES

Competitive pressure

The project assessed entry barriers, supplier power, buyer power, substitution and rivalry, including regulatory clearance and R&D requirements for new entrants.

4Ps

Marketing mix

The plan linked the product's natural positioning with premium-tolerant pricing, promotion through social and influencer channels, and distribution designed for accessibility.

CONTINGENCIES

Operational resilience

Worst-case planning covered raw-material shortages, machinery breakdowns, quality issues, regulatory requirements, and the challenge of building brand awareness and distribution.

Operating design

The proposed factory location was evaluated for sourcing, labor, logistics, utilities and regulation.

1

Source natural inputs

Proximity to suppliers of citronella, lemongrass and other inputs informed the location choice.

2

Manufacture in Bengaluru

Kumbalgodu Industrial Area was proposed for labor access, utilities, licensing fit and separation from residential areas.

3

Distribute through connected routes

Highway and city access were considered to support lower-cost delivery and smoother movement across states.

4

Scale product lines

The project explored multiple form factors and use cases to broaden reach while retaining the natural-product positioning.

Financial plan

The model moved beyond a concept pitch into startup funding, asset, expense, P&L, cash-flow and balance-sheet planning.

TOTAL STARTUP FUNDING REQUIRED
₹80,00,000

The project allocated capital across R&D, raw materials, long-term assets, registration/legal costs, salaries, rent, insurance, utilities and other startup expenses.

R&D alone was modeled at ₹8 lakh, with total startup funding at ₹80 lakh.

3-YEAR MODEL

Path to profitability

The report projected a loss in the first modeled year, followed by positive profit after tax in subsequent years, alongside cash-flow and balance-sheet statements.

Purpose: test whether pricing, operating structure, capital intensity and distribution assumptions could form a coherent venture case.
